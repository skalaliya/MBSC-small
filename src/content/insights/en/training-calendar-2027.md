The full 2027 MBSC Australia training calendar is now published. Teams can plan the year ahead, line up training budgets and secure places early.

## What the calendar covers

The 2027 program runs across six disciplines:

- **Oil & gas:** reservoir engineering, drilling and well design, production technology and artificial lift, cementing and well integrity, well intervention, and HSE leadership.
- **Geoscience:** geoscience fundamentals, structural geology, geophysics, petrophysics, geochemistry, field mapping, geomechanics and advanced GPR and NDT interpretation.
- **Geology:** core logging, geostatistics and resource estimation, mine geology and grade control, domaining and classification, and remote sensing.
- **Mining:** mine planning, ventilation, blast design, ground control, mine-to-mill optimisation, safety leadership and NDT training towards ISO 9712.
- **Digital & AI:** digital oilfield and AI in production, and AI for managers and decision makers.
- **Cross-discipline:** project management, risk management, safety leadership, technical report writing, finance for technical teams, leadership and industrial law.

## How courses are delivered

Courses run in person in Melbourne and Perth, as virtual classrooms open to teams anywhere, or as hybrid programs that combine the two. Any course can also be delivered at your site for a group, outside the published dates.
