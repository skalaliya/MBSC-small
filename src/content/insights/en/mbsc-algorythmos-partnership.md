MBSC Australia has partnered with Algorythmos, an AI consultancy based in Sydney and Paris, as its technology partner for AI and machine learning. The partnership began in September 2026.

## Who does what

MBSC brings the engineering: our consultants and inspectors know the assets, the sites and the standards that mining and energy operators work to. Algorythmos brings the AI engineering: data platforms, model deployment and monitoring, and the automation that keeps machine learning reliable once it is in production.

## What the partnership covers

Algorythmos engineers and operates the AI and machine learning systems behind MBSC's [AI & machine learning for mining](/services/ai-mining) services, including:

- data platforms that bring together sensor, drilling and maintenance data
- model deployment and monitoring (MLOps), so models stay accurate over time
- document intelligence for inspection and maintenance records
- automated workflows with human approval at every critical step

MBSC engineers remain responsible for the engineering. They define each problem, validate the results and sign off every decision that affects a site.

## What it means for clients

Mining and energy operators get field engineering and AI engineering in one engagement, with qualified engineers involved from the first data review to the final recommendation.
